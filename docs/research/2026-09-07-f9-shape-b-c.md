Date: 2026-09-07. Release 0.57.0. Roadmap item F9 (`docs/archive/roadmaps-closed.md`, Phase F).

## Shape B: two builders and a judge sitting

Shape: two `stage: build` lanes on one one-item spec, Sonnet 5 at `hard` and Gemini 3.7 Flash at
`standard`, each with the gate and `test_policy: allow`; `require: any`; a rank collate on Gemini
3.8 Flash with a Sonnet 5 judge, every judge in both lane orders (E4). `self_judging: allow` was
unavoidable: with Anthropic and Google building, Cursor has no schema flag (rank refused) and
Codex is paused, so no vendor with structured output was left to judge cold. The judges were kept
symmetric so each judges its own vendor once.

### Scratch run (`20260907T081211Z-f9-shape-b-scratch`, $0.50)

Spec: `slug()` plus a unittest file on a scratch repository; gate `python3 -m unittest discover`.

| lane | model | gate | cost | patch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| build-sonnet | Sonnet 5 hard | 0 | $0.09 | 960 B |
| build-gemini | Gemini 3.7 Flash | 0 | $0.06 | 1296 B |

The two implementations are the same three lines; Gemini's tests carry more cases. Sitting: the
Gemini judge chose Gemini's build in both orders (reason given: the more thorough tests, 10 vs
9), the Sonnet judge chose Gemini forward and Sonnet reverse (8 vs 7.5, "functionally
equivalent"). Tally 3-1, `agreement: split`, conductor set `strongest` to null and the mission
not ok: a split escalates rather than picking one order's answer. Reading: when both builds are
correct and near-identical, one judge shows self-preference with a defensible reason and the
other shows position bias, and the sitting reports that instead of hiding it.

### Conductor run (`20260907T081340Z-f9-shape-b-source`, $3.48, landed)

Spec: `golden.record` scrubs the mission file's own path (`source` in `mission.json`) to the
placeholder `<source>`, lists it in `golden.json`, replay accepts it, existing fixtures unchanged.

| lane | model | gate | cost | tools | patch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| build-sonnet | Sonnet 5 hard | 0 | $1.79 | 53 | 5907 B |
| build-gemini | Gemini 3.7 Flash | 0 | $0.65 | 156 | 5103 B |

Mechanical ranking put Gemini first (smaller patch, cheaper). Sitting: unanimous for Sonnet, 4-0,
Gemini judge 9.5 vs 4.5, Sonnet judge 8.5 vs 4.5. The reason both gave, confirmed by the lead on
the diffs: Gemini's version rewrites an empty `source` as `<source>` and pops the key when absent
(`snapshot.pop("source", None)`), against the spec's "a missing or null `source` stays as it
is"; Sonnet's scrubs only a non-empty string and adds the placeholder to the manifest only when it
scrubbed. Sonnet's tip gated in a fresh worktree (ruff, 1250 tests, golden check) and was merged
by `conductor land` (gate, golden, attest all ok). The pair plus sitting cost $3.48 against about
$2.80 for one Sonnet build with grace at this cap: the judges picked the build that passed both
the gate and the spec, and the shape cost a quarter more than one build.
